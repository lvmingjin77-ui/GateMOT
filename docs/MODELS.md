# Model zoo

## Coming soon

GateMOT checkpoints for BEE24, SportsMOT, MOT17, and MOT20 will be linked here. Each model will include its training split, selected epoch, configuration, and evaluation command. Checkpoint downloads are not available yet.

The [README](../README.md#results-and-model-zoo) lists the manuscript results. You can already [train and evaluate your own models](../README.md#training) using the provided scripts. The example `model_last.pth` files are generated by training, not downloaded from this page.

Models trained on `halftrain` are evaluated on `halfval`. They are separate from models trained on the complete `train` split for test-set inference. SportsMOT uses its official `train` and `val` directories. These split details will accompany each checkpoint.

## Backbone initialization

The ImageNet initialization weights are available from the DLA project. Download them into `pretrain/` without renaming:

| Backbone | Used by | Download |
|---|---|---|
| DLA-34 | BEE24, MOT17, MOT20, DanceTrack | [dla34-ba72cf86.pth](http://dl.yf.io/dla/models/imagenet/dla34-ba72cf86.pth) |
| DLA-60 | SportsMOT | [dla60-24839fc4.pth](http://dl.yf.io/dla/models/imagenet/dla60-24839fc4.pth) |

Inference with a complete GateMOT checkpoint does not require separate ImageNet weights.
